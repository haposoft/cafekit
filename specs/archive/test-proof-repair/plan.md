# Test proof repair — make cf:test deliver a well-formed test-proof-v1 payload in its final message
Specs-Contract: process-first-ready-v1

## Scope decision (GATE-SCOPE — 2026-10-03)
- Existing:
  - `cf:test` says to return one `test-proof-v1` object to the controller (`packages/spec/src/claude/skills/test/SKILL.md:16-17`, `:80`) and to keep the concise report free of the full JSON (`SKILL.md:164-165`; `references/execution-strategy.md:199-204`). It never says where the payload goes when `/cf:test` runs alone, where the controller reads only the final message. The schema is closed (`execution-strategy.md:92-150`; `task_path` a contained regular flat task at `:105-106`; `provenance` null only before repository identity exists at `:113-115`; branch ids equal the exact unique Named probes at `:136`), and no example shows a `BLOCKED` or `FAIL` payload; a reachability `FAIL` is an attempted `FAIL` (`:163-164`).
  - Measured, `specs/test-eval-baseline` and `specs/test-eval-hard` (140 runs): a payload was printed in 2–10 of 10 runs per cell (41 of 80 baseline runs said it was ready without printing it); printed payloads were invalid mostly on `FAIL`/`BLOCKED` runs — `target` a string, branches holding only `{id, status}`, counts that do not sum, a wrong digest (`kho-khong-cham-code-sonnet` 2 of 6 valid); one report dropped the `**Status:**` label of the template (`SKILL.md:149`).
  - `packages/spec/scripts/run-skill-self-tests.mjs` pins the test skill (`TEST_PLAN_NATIVE_PATHS` at `:2117-2122`; clauses at `:2208`, `:2241-2245`; a forbidden-wording regex at `:2271`; mutations at `:2327`, `:2352`; each anchor replaced exactly once at `:2583-2589`); `packages/spec/bin/__tests__/develop-contract.test.js:995-1020` and `codex-native.test.js:2740-2780` pin `test-proof-v1` in the installed runner and review.
  - `evals/test/` measures it: `check-payload.mjs` (`validate()` exported; delivered and valid per cell), `compare.mjs`, `budget.mjs`, `run.sh --plugin-name cf`; the cells `base-*` and `kho-*` are the before-numbers.
- Minimum change: for a target whose task can be read, the skill's final message becomes the concise report, then one fenced `json` payload block that follows the report and is not part of it, with a checked example of a `BLOCKED` payload and the `FAIL` variants; self-test clauses pin it; the after-cells measure four cases on both models against the before-cells.
- Expansion signals: none.
- User decision: KEEP — four cases measured; the payload goes in the final message; measure without a pass threshold; cap $100.

## Out of scope
- The `test-runner` agent and `code-auditor`; the payload schema itself; other skills; the installed `.claude/` copy; the cases not measured here; a grader-repair round; pushing unless asked.
